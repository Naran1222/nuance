MISSION OVERVIEW

SPHEREx creates a complete near-infrared map of the sky every six months. It captures 102 colors, earning it the nickname "the mantis shrimp of telescopes.

Over its two-year mission, it will survey over 450 million galaxies, determining their distances by color. All data becomes public within 60 days, allowing you to work with it on an equal footing with scientists.
